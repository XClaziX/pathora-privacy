Data deletion — PATHORA

Android app com.clxon24.pathora · Last updated: 19 July 2026

This page explains how to delete PATHORA data and how to request deletion from the developer. PATHORA does not run its own player account server. Most gameplay data lives only on your device.

1. Delete data yourself (fastest)

  1. On your Android device open Settings → Apps → PATHORA.
  2. Choose Storage → Clear data (or uninstall the app).
  3. Local progress, settings, and coins stored by PATHORA are removed immediately.
Uninstalling PATHORA or clearing app data deletes the developer-controlled local save right away. No waiting period.

2. Optional Play Games cloud save

If you connected Google Play Games in PATHORA Settings:

  • Open PATHORA → Settings and disconnect / sign out of Play Games if available, or
  • Manage / delete Play Games saved games in your Google / Play Games account settings.

Cloud snapshots are stored by Google Play Games, not on a PATHORA server. After a deletion request we will also delete any cloud snapshot we can access for your request (when Play Games is enabled for the app), typically within 30 days.

3. What we delete vs what we cannot delete

  • Deleted by you / us: local PATHORA save data; support messages you sent us; Play Games snapshots we can remove for your request.
  • Not controlled by us: Google AdMob advertising identifiers and ad logs (handled by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy); Android Auto Backup copies managed by Google/your device backup.
  • Purchase records: Google Play keeps purchase history for legal/billing reasons; we do not store your card details.

4. Submit a deletion request

Open a request with the button below. Please include:

  • That the request is for PATHORA (com.clxon24.pathora)
  • Whether you used Play Games cloud sync (yes/no)
  • Any support emails you previously sent (so we can delete those threads)

Request data deletion

We aim to confirm and complete developer-side deletion within 30 days of a clear request. Local device data is already gone after uninstall / clear data.

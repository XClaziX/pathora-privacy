2. Overview

PATHORA (“the App”, package ID com.clxon24.pathora) is an Android match-3 puzzle game. The App does not require an account or registration. Gameplay progress is stored only locally on your device (Android DataStore). The App does not create, share, or upload user-generated content to any server operated by us.

To support development, the App may display advertising via Google AdMob and use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect advertising consent. As a result, personal data (in particular advertising identifiers) may be processed by Google and connected ad networks.

3. What data is processed?

Data typePurposeController
Advertising ID, device identifiers Personalized/non-personalized ads, fraud prevention Google AdMob
Ad interactions (impressions, clicks) Ad serving, billing Google AdMob
Consent status Storing your advertising consent choice Google UMP (locally + potentially Google servers)
Local save data (level progress, settings) Storing gameplay progress Local device only

The App itself does not collect names, email addresses, location, or payment information. If you contact us voluntarily (e.g. for support), we process contact details solely to respond.

4. Google AdMob & advertising

The App may show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may process device identifiers and usage data. More information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from partner apps

Interstitials appear only after several completed levels and respect a cooldown. Rewarded ads are optional.

5. Consent via UMP

On first launch (and for relevant users in the EEA/UK/Switzerland and other regions determined by Google), a consent form may appear via UMP. You can change this in Settings → Privacy options. If consent fails technically, the game still starts — personalized ads are not requested until consent is obtained.

6. Children / target audience

PATHORA is not specifically directed at children under 13. Family/child-directed programs may require additional compliance (e.g. COPPA, GDPR-K).

7. Data retention & deletion

  • Progress and settings are stored only on device and removed on uninstall or clearing app data.
  • We do not operate a server for personal player data. Requests about Google ad data should go to Google.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection regarding personal data we process directly (e.g. support messages). Contact via GitHub Issues.

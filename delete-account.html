Account & data deletion

PATHORA — Match 3 Puzzle (com.clxon24.pathora)
Developer: clxon24 · Updated: July 19, 2026

How to delete local game data yourself, and how to request deletion of optional Play Games–linked cloud progress.

1. Delete local data (without deleting an account)

  1. Android: App info → Storage → Clear data, or
  2. Uninstall the app.

This removes locally stored progress, settings, and coins. It does not delete your Google account.

2. Request Play Games / linked data deletion

  1. Email cripz-yt@hotmail.com
  2. Subject: PATHORA Account Deletion
  3. Include your Play Games / Google account email (or player ID) and your deletion request

We typically process requests within 30 days and unlink/delete Play Games–related game progress we control where technically possible. Data held by Google (Play, Ads, Billing) follows Google’s controls and retention.

3. What is deleted / what may remain

  • Deleted / unlinked: optional Play Games synced progress we control
  • Local: no local game data after clear data / uninstall
  • Short retention: support email until the request is handled
  • Google ads / purchases: managed via your Google account
